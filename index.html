AVC-Reward

Event-Centric Audio-Video Consistency Evaluation

A trainable reward model that evaluates how sounds and visual events fit together—even when a scene contains several events, layered audio, or valid off-screen sounds.

Read the paper ↗ Explore case studies ↓

A general-purpose reward signal for complex, jointly generated audio-visual scenes.

Overview

Evaluate the events, not just the clip.

Modern audio-video generation can combine multiple visible events, physical sounds, speech, narration, and music. A single clip-level similarity score can hide a local timing error, mix audio layers with different roles, or incorrectly penalize a plausible sound from outside the frame.

AVC-Reward evaluates these relationships at the event level. It separates audio and visual events, verifies how they relate, and combines the evidence into a structured consistency judgment. The resulting reward model aligns closely with human judgments and can also guide generation during reinforcement learning.

Method overview

From event extraction to a useful judgment.

The framework separates events first, checks their cross-modal relationships, then scores consistency from coarse to fine.

01

Extract events

Analyze audio and video independently, preserving distinct sounds and visible actions.

02

Verify relations

Check event presence, timing, attributes, and whether unmatched sounds plausibly come from off screen.

03

Judge at three levels

Assess narrative harmony, event-level synchronization, and fine-grained attribute alignment.
