A clap of thunder. At first, it exploded across the open sky, a full-spectrum, intensely crackling roar. As the sound reached a dense forest, the sound waves were filtered and absorbed by countless tree trunks, leaves, and the soft ground; the high-frequency crackles vanished, leaving only a deep, rolling rumble. As the sound continued to travel and entered the mouth of a cave halfway up a mountainside, it was once again focused and colored, transforming into a long, resonant "whooshing" sound with a unique tonal quality.
